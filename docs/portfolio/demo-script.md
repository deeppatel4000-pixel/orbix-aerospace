# ORBIX 60-second demonstration script

## 0–10 seconds — Introduce ORBIX

Open `/showcase` on the hero and mission gallery.

> ORBIX is an Advanced Aerospace Engineering Laboratory that connects mission inputs, typed analysis,
> reports, visualization, and engineering review in one educational platform.

Name the key boundary: physics stays in pure TypeScript; the interface communicates completed
outputs.

## 10–25 seconds — Create and analyze a mission

Open Engineering Laboratory, load Lunar Transfer Concept, and show the populated Mission Profile.

> Educational presets feed the same typed contracts as a custom scenario. The mission profile
> orchestrates existing orbital analysis and delta-v budgeting without moving equations into React.

Briefly point to the total delta-v, transfer context, and preserved source analysis.

## 25–40 seconds — Visualize in Mission Control

Enter Mission Control and open the Orbit workspace.

> Mission Control receives the completed mission object. The orbital paths, telemetry, timeline, and
> replay are communication layers rather than a second simulation engine.

Show one workspace transition and the persistent status context.

## 40–55 seconds — Review engineering decisions

Open Design Review or Trade Study.

> ORBIX keeps assumptions and limitations visible. Its review tools compare supplied mission and TPS
> outputs without assigning artificial feasibility, safety, or certification claims.

If demonstrating reentry instead, show the Reentry Demonstrator's heating, deceleration, and TPS
context.

## 55–60 seconds — Close on the portfolio story

Return to Briefing or Showcase.

> ORBIX demonstrates aerospace systems thinking, maintainable full-stack architecture, automated
> validation, and professional technical communication in one production-deployed project.

End on the live URL and GitHub repository. Do not rush into another module after the closing frame.
